Privacy Policy ♡

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Welcome to My Little Pink World ♡. Your privacy matters to me. This page explains what information may be processed when you visit my website or leave a message in the guestbook.

1. Who runs this website?

My Little Pink World ♡ is a personal blog.

Website: https://mylittlepinkworld.github.io/

Contact: Jojocl392@gmail.com

Please use this email address if you have a question about privacy or your personal information.

2. Information collected

Guestbook

If you leave a guestbook message, the website stores the name and message you submit, along with the date and time of submission.

Messages are reviewed before they appear publicly. Only approved messages are displayed on the website.

Please do not include private information, such as your home address, phone number, passwords, or other sensitive details, in a guestbook message.

Website administration

The website uses an authenticated administration area to manage blog posts, articles, links, images, and other site content. Administrative account information is handled by the authentication provider.

Technical information

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages and uses Supabase for certain website features and data storage. These services may process technical information as part of providing and securing their services. Please refer to their own privacy notices for further information.

3. Why information is used

Information submitted through the guestbook is used to display messages that have been approved.

Administrative information is used to secure access to the website editor and manage website content.

Information is not intentionally collected for advertising or sold for advertising purposes.

4. Public guestbook messages

If your guestbook message is approved, the name and message you submitted will be visible to other visitors.

Please only submit information you are comfortable sharing publicly. If you would like your message removed, contact me at Jojocl392@gmail.com.

5. Hosting and third-party services

  • GitHub Pages — hosting the website.
  • Supabase — authentication, database storage, and website functionality.

These providers process information according to their own terms and privacy policies. Their handling of technical information may depend on their services and settings.

6. Data retention

Guestbook messages may remain stored while they are needed to operate the guestbook. Approved messages may remain publicly visible until they are removed.

You can contact me to request the removal of a message or ask a question about stored information. Some technical records may be retained separately by the service providers according to their own policies.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on the applicable law and circumstances, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, correct it, request its deletion, restrict certain processing, or object to certain processing.

To make a request, contact Jojocl392@gmail.com.

If you are in Belgium, you can also find information about your rights from the Belgian Data Protection Authority: dataprotectionauthority.be.

8. Children's privacy

This website is intended to be a friendly personal blog. Please do not submit sensitive personal information through the guestbook.

If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern about information submitted by a child, please contact me at Jojocl392@gmail.com.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the website's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page, together with its update date.
