If you submit a guestbook message, the name and message you provide are stored, together with the submission date and time. Messages are reviewed before they appear publicly. Only approved messages are displayed on the website.

Guestbook information is used to manage and display messages that have been approved. Authentication information is used to secure the administration area. Technical information may be processed by the hosting and service providers to operate and protect their services.
