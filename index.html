Privacy Policy ♡

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Welcome to My Little Pink World ♡. Your privacy matters to me. This page explains what information may be processed when you visit my website or leave a guestbook message.

1. Who runs this website?

My Little Pink World ♡ is a personal blog.

Website: mylittlepinkworld.github.io

Contact: Jojocl392@gmail.com

2. Information collected

If you leave a guestbook message, your submitted name and message, together with the submission date and time, are stored. Messages are reviewed before appearing publicly.

Please do not share your home address, phone number, passwords or other sensitive information in the guestbook.

The website also uses an authenticated administration area to manage content. GitHub Pages hosts the website and Supabase provides database, authentication and other website features. These providers may process technical information.

3. Why information is used

Guestbook information is used to display approved messages. Administrative information is used to secure the website editor and manage content. Information is not intentionally collected for advertising or sold for advertising purposes.

4. Public guestbook messages

If approved, your submitted name and message can be seen by other visitors. Contact Jojocl392@gmail.com if you would like to request removal.

5. Hosting and third-party services

GitHub Pages hosts this website. Supabase provides authentication, database storage and website functionality. Please consult their privacy policies for details about their handling of information.

6. Data retention

Guestbook messages may remain stored while needed to operate the guestbook. Approved messages may remain visible until removed. Service providers may retain some technical records under their own policies.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law and circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct or request deletion of your personal information, and other rights.

Contact Jojocl392@gmail.com with a request. In Belgium, you can also visit the Belgian Data Protection Authority.

8. Children's privacy

Please do not submit sensitive personal information through the guestbook. Parents or guardians with concerns can contact Jojocl392@gmail.com.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the website or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on the full Privacy Policy page.
